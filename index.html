01 / Why most doctors' YouTube stalls

Your videos get watched. Then the viewer leaves for good.

PROBLEM 01
Uploads stall

Clinic days win. A month goes by without a video, and the channel loses the momentum it took a year to build.

PROBLEM 02
Good videos, no clicks

The advice is excellent. The title and thumbnail don't make anyone press play, so the people searching never see it.

PROBLEM 03
No way back to you

Someone watches, nods, and closes the tab. No email, no follow-up. They book with whoever they remember.

The content isn't the problem. The system around it is.
